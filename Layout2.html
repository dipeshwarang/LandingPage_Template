Hazelnut Cake

It is a moist cake, with the nutty flavour of hazelnuts enhanced by a spoonful of unsweetened cocoa powder.



3 Tier Cake

A 3-tier cake consists of three stacked layers, often used for weddings, birthdays, or special events. Each tier can have different sizes and flavors, typically serving 60-74 people.



Coconut Cake

Coconut cake is a moist, fluffy dessert made with coconut milk or shredded coconut, offering a rich tropical flavor. It's often layered with coconut frosting and topped with toasted coconut flakes.



Apple Crumb

Apple crumb cake is a moist, spiced cake layered with tender apple slices and topped with a buttery cinnamon crumb topping.


Oreos Cake

Oreo cake is a rich, chocolatey dessert made with crushed Oreo cookies layered into the batter and frosting. It's creamy, crunchy, and perfect for Oreo lovers of all ages.


Blackberry Cake

Blackberry cake is a soft, fruity dessert made with fresh or pureed blackberries for a sweet-tart flavor. Often paired with lemon or vanilla frosting, it's perfect for summer occasions.
